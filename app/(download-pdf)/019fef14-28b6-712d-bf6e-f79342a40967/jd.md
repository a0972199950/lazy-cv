# Full Stack Software Engineer I — Booking.com (Booking.com for Business)

**Location:** Singapore, Singapore
**Source URL:** https://jobs.booking.com/booking/jobs/29613?lang=en-us

## About the team

Booking.com for Business is a travel product designed for the needs of small and medium size enterprises. It lets companies book and manage business trips — stays, flights, and car rental — in one place. Built in partnership with Serko Ltd. Team focus: rapid experimentation and general agility, large amount of R&D, large amount of integrations with a diverse array of services and components within and outside of Booking.com. Some travel required throughout the year.

## Role Description

As a Software Engineer I (FullStack), you will be responsible for the implementation, performance, and scaling of the public website as well as internal systems. Work independently and be responsible for making technical decisions within a team.

## Key Job Responsibilities and Duties

- Rapidly implementing next-generation scalable, flexible, and high-performance systems
- Solving issues with the site and internal systems, prioritizing based on customer impact
- Acting as an intermediary for problems, with both technical and non-technical audiences
- Testing — running thousands of experiments at one time on one of the most advanced A/B testing platforms in the industry
- Advocate a collaborative working environment — make "my ideas" into "our ideas"
- Identify, foster, and proactively help colleagues grow professionally, taking a significant role in onboarding new team members
- Contributing to the growth of Booking.com through interviewing, onboarding, or other recruitment efforts

## Qualifications & Skills

- 2-5 years of experience in software development with any of the following server-side programming languages like Java/Python/Perl & web technologies like NodeJs/React/Vue/jQuery/HTML/CSS
- Experience with MySQL or similar RDBMS
- Experience working on products that impact a large customer base is an advantage
- Self-starter, highly motivated, team-player
- Excellent communication skills; written and spoken
- 'Can do' attitude, proactive not reactive
- Preferably a university degree in Software Engineering

## Benefits

Hybrid working including flexible working arrangements, up to 20 days/year working from abroad (home country), product discounts, Genius Level 3 status.
