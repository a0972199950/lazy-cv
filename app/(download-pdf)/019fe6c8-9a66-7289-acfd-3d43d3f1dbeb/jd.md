# Software Engineer, Payments

**Company**: Stripe
**Location**: Singapore
**Employment Type**: Full time
**URL**: https://stripe.com/careers/listing/software-engineer-payments/7529787

## About the team
Payments is Stripe's flagship product. Our payments platform processes trillions of dollars annually. The team focuses on expanding Stripe's global payments network and delivering reliability and performance across the system.

## Main Responsibilities
- Design, build, and maintain APIs and services across engineering teams
- Work end-to-end on problems spanning multiple systems and technologies
- Collaborate globally with other engineering teams
- Uphold engineering standards and improve processes

## Minimum Requirements
- 5+ years of professional software development experience
- Bachelor's degree in technical field or equivalent experience
- Experience on multi-team projects
- Strong communication and collaboration skills
- Ability to navigate ambiguous situations independently
- High code quality standards

## Preferred Qualifications
- Background with financial product companies
- User-facing or cross-functional product roles

## Compensation Range
SGD 122,400 - SGD 270,000 annually (primary location). Range may vary by location and will narrow during interviews based on experience and qualifications.

## Benefits
Equity, bonuses, retirement plans, health benefits, and wellness stipends (specifics vary by location).

## Office Expectations
At least 50% in-office time monthly in local office or with users (varies by role and team).
